RFP Monster is a GenAI workflow (RAG) that automatically generates answers to the questions potential clients pose in RFPs, then categorizes the LLM's responses with predictive AI models.

It won 2nd place in DataRobot's company-wide hackathon, and was presented at the company's internal summer meeting and at its [Fall launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wf0iF5akLOA).
